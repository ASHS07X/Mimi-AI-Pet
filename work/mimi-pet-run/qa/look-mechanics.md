# Mimi look mechanics

Mimi’s oversized white head, small brown ears, pink bow at the upper viewer-right, large glossy dark-brown eyes with white highlights, rosy cheeks, small pink mouth, and brown bowtie define the design. The eyes are complete glossy eye surfaces: preserve their dark shape and highlights together, with no new pupils or replacement eyes.

Keep the feet and lower body anchored. Turn or pitch the head and face together for gaze directions, letting the far eye foreshorten naturally. The ears and bow follow the head and remain attached; the bow may lag slightly. For up/down, pitch the head while keeping the torso upright. Avoid whole-character rotation, broad warping, added accessories, or background effects.
